# Internet of Things (IoT)

**IoT** means physical devices – sensors, machines, smart meters, cameras – connected to the internet and sending data.

**Real-life comparison:** A smart thermostat at home that you can check from your phone is IoT. AWS IoT services do the same for thousands or millions of devices.

## Services in this category

| Service | Level | What it does |
|---------|-------|--------------|
| [AWS IoT Core](iot-core.md) | 🟡 | Connects devices to AWS securely |
| [AWS IoT Greengrass](iot-greengrass.md) | 🔴 | Runs AWS code on devices themselves |
| [AWS IoT SiteWise](iot-sitewise.md) | 🔴 | Collects and monitors data from industrial equipment |
