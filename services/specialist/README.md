# Specialist services (blockchain, quantum, satellite)

These services are for very specific jobs. Most cloud engineers rarely use them, but it's good to know they exist – they sometimes appear in certification exams.

| Service | Level | What it does |
|---------|-------|--------------|
| [Amazon Managed Blockchain](managed-blockchain.md) | 🔴 | Run blockchain networks without managing the servers |
| [Amazon Braket](braket.md) | 🔴 | Try quantum computing |
| [AWS Ground Station](ground-station.md) | 🔴 | Control satellites and download their data |
