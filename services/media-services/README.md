# Media services

Services for **converting**, **streaming** and **processing** video.

**Real-life comparison:** A TV studio needs editors to convert footage into the right formats, broadcast equipment for live shows, and camera feeds coming in. These services do those jobs in the cloud.

## Services in this category

| Service | Level | What it does |
|---------|-------|--------------|
| [AWS Elemental MediaConvert](mediaconvert.md) | 🟡 | Converts video files into formats for phones, TVs and the web |
| [Amazon IVS](ivs.md) | 🟡 | Add live video streaming to your app |
| [Amazon Kinesis Video Streams](kinesis-video-streams.md) | 🔴 | Streams video from cameras into AWS for processing |
| [Amazon Elastic Transcoder](elastic-transcoder.md) ⛔ | 🟡 | Old video converter – shut down November 2025 |
